## Context and problem

One product with two very different users: the customer who requests a repair and the provider who handles it. Each one needs their own flow without duplicating the application.

## My role and scope

I built the frontend of both flows: service requests, status tracking, profiles and ratings.

## How it works

Each type of user has their own navigation: the customer uses a classic bottom bar and the provider a floating bar.

### Customer app

- **Home.** Shows the active request and a shortcut to create a service.
- **Create service.** Service type, saved address and a description of the problem. If there are no addresses, it offers to add one right there.
- **Profile.** Personal information, payment methods, addresses, settings, privacy and help.

### Provider app

- **Availability.** The provider holds a button to go active or inactive, and a timer shows how long they have been active.
- **Statistics.** Income, completed and active services, and average per service, with a period filter and an income chart.
- **Ratings.** Average, distribution by stars and recent customer comments.

## Technical decisions

- **Separate flows per user type** on a single codebase, each with its own navigation and screens.
- **Status tracking for every request**, visible to both the customer and the provider.
- **State with Redux Toolkit**, REST API with Axios and JWT authentication with protected routes.
- **Responsive, mobile-first interface with Tailwind CSS.**

## Outcome

A PWA with two complete experiences, the customer's and the provider's, built on the same product and ready for its pre-launch phase.
