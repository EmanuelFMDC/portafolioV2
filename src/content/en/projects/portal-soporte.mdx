## Context and problem

An internal dashboard where client companies open technical support and design tickets and follow their status until they are completed.

## My role and scope

Frontend in React and TypeScript, with Redux for state, React Router for navigation and Axios to consume the API. Tests with Vitest and Playwright.

## How it works

- **Sign-in with the company's Google account.** Login uses Google Workspace SSO. A company without access can request registration from the same screen.
- **Ticket table with filters.** Filter by number, user, description, type and status (open, in progress, completed), sorted by last update.
- **Guided ticket creation.** The form changes by type: design (print or digital) or technical support. It accepts evidence by dragging images, PDFs or Word documents up to 10 MB.
- **Account settings.** Dark mode, service expiry, payments, plan scope and usage reports.

## Technical decisions

- **State with Redux** and routing with React Router.
- **Forms that adapt to the type of request**, with drag-and-drop evidence upload and format and size validation.
- **Testing** with Vitest for the logic and Playwright for the main flows.

## Outcome

Client companies open and follow their requests in one place, with status filters and attached evidence, and sign in with the Google account they already use.
