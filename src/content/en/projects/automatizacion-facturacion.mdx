## Context and problem

The sales and purchasing teams invoiced by hand in Excel. The process was slow and prone to data-entry errors.

## My role and scope

I built a web application for recording expenses with Google Apps Script, with forms, validations and a supplier catalog.

## How it works

When recording an expense you choose between two capture types.

- **XML invoice.** You upload the CFDI (the Mexican electronic invoice) and the application fills in the UUID, the supplier, the amounts and the line items by itself. Nobody retypes them.
- **Cash payment.** Manual entry for petty cash, travel expenses or receipts without XML, with required fields and tax information as optional.

## Outcome

It replaced the manual Excel work of the sales and purchasing teams: the invoice XML fills in the data by itself, and manual entry is left for expenses without a receipt.
